# 💡 E-Commerce Customer & Order Analytics — Key Insights

## 1. Overall Order Activity

- The dataset contains **1,000 orders** from **300 registered customers**.
- **285 customers** placed at least one order, resulting in a **95.0% customer activation rate**.
- The analysis focuses on order activity, customer behavior, geographic distribution, repeat purchases, and retention.

### Business Insight

The high 95.0% activation rate indicates that most registered customers have made at least one purchase. However, the remaining inactive customers represent an opportunity for targeted conversion campaigns.

---

## 2. Order Status Analysis

- **805 orders** were completed successfully.
- **103 orders** were cancelled.
- **92 orders** were returned.
- Completed orders represent **80.5%** of total orders.
- Cancelled orders represent **10.3%** of total orders.
- Returned orders represent **9.2%** of total orders.

### Business Insight

The majority of orders were completed successfully. However, the 10.3% cancellation rate and 9.2% return rate indicate that order cancellations and returns should be monitored to identify possible operational or customer-experience issues.

---

## 3. Monthly Order Trends

- Order activity varied across the analysis period.
- The highest number of orders occurred in **October 2024**, with **102 orders**.
- The lowest number of orders occurred in **July 2024**, with **66 orders**.
- This represents a difference of **36 orders** between the highest and lowest months.

### Business Insight

Monthly order trends can help the business identify periods of high and low customer activity and support better planning of marketing campaigns and operational resources.

---

## 4. Geographic Analysis

The top countries by order volume were:

| Rank | Country | Orders |
|---|---|---:|
| 1 | Spain | 225 |
| 2 | Italy | 223 |
| 3 | France | 194 |
| 4 | Morocco | 190 |
| 5 | Germany | 168 |

- **Spain** recorded the highest order volume with **225 orders**.
- **Italy** followed closely with **223 orders**.
- France recorded **194 orders**, while Morocco recorded **190 orders**.
- Germany recorded **168 orders**.

### Business Insight

Spain and Italy are the strongest markets based on order activity. These markets could receive greater marketing attention, while lower-volume markets can be evaluated for opportunities to improve customer engagement.

---

## 5. Customer Activity

- There are **300 registered customers** in the dataset.
- **285 customers** placed at least one order.
- The customer activation rate is **95.0%**.
- **15 registered customers** did not place an order during the analysis period.

### Business Insight

The 15 customers who registered but did not place an order represent a potential conversion opportunity. Targeted onboarding emails, promotional offers, or reminders could be used to encourage their first purchase.

---

## 6. Repeat Customer Analysis

- **246 customers** placed more than one order.
- **39 customers** placed exactly one order.
- The repeat customer rate among active customers is **86.3%**.
- The repeat customer rate compared with all registered customers is **82.0%**.
- The highest-frequency customer placed **9 orders**.

### Business Insight

The high repeat customer rate indicates strong customer engagement among active customers. Repeat customers can be targeted with loyalty programs, personalized recommendations, and exclusive offers.

---

## 7. Customer Order Frequency

- Customer order frequency varies across the customer base.
- **246 customers** placed multiple orders.
- **39 customers** placed only one order.
- The highest-frequency customer, **Customer 99**, placed **9 orders**.
- Several customers placed 7–8 orders during the analysis period.

### Business Insight

High-frequency customers represent an important engaged customer group. The business can use loyalty rewards and personalized campaigns to maintain their engagement and encourage continued purchasing.

---

## 8. Customer Retention Analysis

Retention was analyzed based on whether an active customer made another purchase within 30, 60, or 90 days of their first order.

### 30-Day Retention

- **63 customers** made another purchase within 30 days.
- 30-day repeat rate: **22.1%**

### 60-Day Retention

- **113 customers** made another purchase within 60 days.
- 60-day repeat rate: **39.6%**

### 90-Day Retention

- **149 customers** made another purchase within 90 days.
- 90-day repeat rate: **52.3%**

### Business Insight

The repeat-purchase rate increases from **22.1% within 30 days** to **52.3% within 90 days**. This indicates that a significant portion of customers return after their initial purchase, although many customers do not make a second purchase within the first 30 days.

The business could therefore focus on early post-purchase engagement to encourage customers to return sooner.

---

## 9. Customer Engagement Segmentation

Customers were analyzed using recency and frequency metrics.

The analysis can be used to identify groups such as:

- **Loyal Customers**
- **Regular Customers**
- **New/One-Time Customers**
- **At-Risk Customers**

### Business Insight

Different customer groups require different engagement strategies:

| Customer Segment | Recommended Strategy |
|---|---|
| Loyal Customers | Loyalty rewards and exclusive offers |
| Regular Customers | Personalized recommendations |
| New/One-Time Customers | Follow-up campaigns and incentives |
| At-Risk Customers | Re-engagement campaigns |

## 11. Key Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following actions can be considered:

1. **Focus on repeat customers** through loyalty programs and personalized offers.
2. **Target one-time customers** to encourage a second purchase.
3. **Improve early retention** by engaging customers during the first 30 days after their initial purchase.
4. **Focus marketing efforts on high-performing markets**, particularly Spain and Italy.
5. **Monitor cancellation and return rates** to identify potential operational issues.
6. **Target inactive registered customers** with onboarding and conversion campaigns.
7. **Use customer segmentation** to provide different strategies for loyal, regular, new, and at-risk customers.

The available order dataset does not contain:

- Product IDs linked to orders
- Quantity
- Price

- Profit

Therefore, product-level sales and financial analysis were not performed.
